My Creations

Some command block stuff I've made. Each one is a single item: give it to yourself, place it in creative with command blocks on, and it sets itself up.

You can also grab these in game on the NBT Archives. Join play.nbtarchives.net, head to archive_new and run /search PiggoPotamus. There's a lot more of my stuff there than on this page.

Playable Chess

Full chess you can play in Minecraft. Place it in your mainhand for a 2×2 board, or your offhand for a chess table.
