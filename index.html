Troubleshooting

Colours look wrong, or there are too few colours

Try these steps in order to leave more room for colour detail:

  1. Use Compact mode. It makes a smaller item than Fast mode.
  2. Increase Max item size if your server allows it. Recommended max: 930 KB. Large items can be rejected and get you kicked.
  3. Lower Target width in pixels. Fewer pixels leave more room for colours. You can also reduce Max pixels per frame.
  4. Increase Ticks per frame to store fewer frames over the same clip, or reduce Frame cap. Motion becomes less smooth; reducing Frame cap also shortens playback.
  5. Increase Target number of colours, or leave it blank for Auto. The item-size limit may still reduce the final count.
  6. Increase Conversion quality to High or Very high for a more thorough search.

Fine details are missing

Increase Target width in pixels and Max pixels per frame if the item-size limit allows it. Try a higher conversion quality.

Edges are blurred/blending colour

Choose Pixel art under Advanced settings → Resize filter. It cannot remove blending already in the source.

The GIF plays too quickly

The frame cap drops frames across the whole clip and shortens playback. Increase Frame cap under Advanced settings to keep more frames and more of the original duration.

Conversion takes too long

Choose Medium or Low conversion quality. Reducing pixels or frames also reduces conversion work.

Minecraft rejects the item or disconnects you

Lower Max item size and convert again. Large items may exceed your server’s limits. Use Item Editor, Meteor Client or a datapack if the command is too long to paste.

The image item does nothing

Give yourself the MID engine item and place it first in creative mode. Ensure command blocks are enabled and you have permission to use them.

Using MID

MID means Minecraft Image Display. It uses command blocks to display images and play GIFs in Minecraft Java Edition.

  1. Download the MID engine using the section above the converter. Import the engine item, then place it in your world first.
  2. Choose an image, GIF or video. Adjust the settings and press Convert. Use Source and Converted to check the result.
  3. Download your image item. We recommend SNBT with Item Editor in creative mode.

Compact makes a smaller item but takes longer to load in-game. Fast makes a bigger item that loads faster initially. Smaller pixel, colour or size limits can reduce detail.
